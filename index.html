A little about me

I came to instructional design from the front line. Years of handling real customer cases taught me where training lets people down, and what it feels like to be handed a process you never got to practice.

So I build the training I wish I’d had: hands-on, scenario-driven, and a little bit funny. Nobody remembers a slide deck, but everybody remembers the time they got it wrong somewhere safe.
