Case study

Fourth Wish Animal Adoption

An interactive case review sandbox that let new hires practice on a real CRM workflow — before a real case was ever on the line.

Fourth Wish Animal Adoption is an animal rescue with a growing intake team and a CRM most new hires didn't feel confident in until they'd already handled a few dozen real cases. I designed and built the org's first fully interactive practice environment — a click-through sandbox where trainees search, review, and decide on adoption cases exactly as they would in the live system, without the risk of a real case hanging in the balance.

The problem

New case reviewers were learning the CRM by doing — reviewing real applications, real animals, real families waiting on an answer. That worked fine for the straightforward cases. It broke down on the edge cases: an incomplete vet history, a flagged prior return, an applicant whose paperwork didn't quite match their interview notes. Those are exactly the situations where a mistake matters most, and exactly the situations no one felt ready for until they'd already been thrown into one.

The approach

Rather than another video walkthrough or a slide deck of screenshots, I built a sandbox: a fully interactive, safe-to-fail copy of the CRM's case review flow. Trainees don't watch someone else review a case — they review one themselves, with real consequences removed but the actual mechanics, the actual decision points, and the actual moments of “wait, what do I do with this?” left in.

The walkthrough

Take Apollo — a three-year-old lab mix with a vet history that stops abruptly eight months ago, and an applicant who mentions in passing that they already have “two dogs, one very large cat, and a bearded dragon named Steve.”

Nothing about the mechanics changes for a case like Apollo's versus a routine one. That consistency was the point — the sandbox needed to feel like the real tool, personality and all, so the muscle memory trainees build actually transfers.

Reflection

This was the first interactive — not just clickable-slide — training environment built for the CRM at my employer. The biggest challenge: I'd had access to Claude Design for less than a month before building it, so a good chunk of the six weeks was spent teaching myself the tool alongside designing the actual case review flow. The payoff was worth it — this kind of environment brings a tactile, hands-on piece into training that a slide deck simply can't replicate.
